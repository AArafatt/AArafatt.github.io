## Mentoring

<p style="text-align:justify;color:black; font-family:Georgia">
I have mentored peers and junior students in research projects related to machine learning, natural language processing, computer vision, and multimodal systems. My mentoring approach emphasizes hands-on guidance, sharing resources, and providing constructive feedback on experiment design, model development, and research writing.
</p>

## Collaboration

<p style="text-align:justify;color:black; font-family:Georgia">
