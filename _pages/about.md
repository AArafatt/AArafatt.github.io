<span style="color:black; font-family:Georgia">Greetings! 👋</span>

<p style="text-align:justify; color:black; font-family:Georgia">
I am Md. Arafat Alam Khandaker, an AI researcher and software engineer passionate about building intelligent systems that create real-world impact.
</p>

<p style="text-align:justify;color:black; font-family:Georgia">
I am currently working as a Software Engineer (AI/ML) at Cloudly Infotech Limited, where I design and develop AI-driven solutions, including HR intelligence agents and log-analysis platforms. Previously, I served as a Research Assistant at the Applied Intelligent Systems and Information Processing (AISIP) Lab, Ahsanullah University of Science and Technology. I hold a Bachelor's degree in Computer Science and Engineering from Ahsanullah University of Science and Technology.
</p>

<p style="text-align:justify;color:black; font-family:Georgia">
My research focuses on <b>Natural Language Processing, Multimodal AI, Explainable Deep Learning, and Social Good applications</b>. I have worked on projects involving toxicity and hate speech detection in Bangla, sarcasm and bullying mitigation, medical image analysis, agricultural disease detection, and multimodal fake news detection, with several international publications in reputed journals and conferences.
</p>
